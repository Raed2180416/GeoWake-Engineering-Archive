# GeoWake engineering archive

**A Flutter/Android transit-alarm codebase, with its development history, tests and underground-tracking experiments, prepared for AI training and software-engineering evaluation.**

GeoWake combines destination search, route selection, pre-boarding warnings,
distance/time/stops alarms, deviation detection, rerouting, background recovery,
native Android alarm delivery and an Express backend. Its underground work
explores route-constrained EKF estimation, IMU signals, ZUPT and station
association. The founder stopped product development when the cost of obtaining
a suitable calibration and validation dataset became prohibitive. Stop anchoring
remained experimental.

The engineering record is the asset: working components, interactions between
systems, genuine debugging changes, rejected assumptions and unfinished plans.
The project was solo-led and AI-assisted. No further product-development or
launch engagement is included in the proposed sale.

## Read the history accurately

Historical product, architecture and research documents remain traceable through
[the archive index](docs/archive/README.md). Their old prices, launch plans and
performance aspirations are historical context. New explanations and packaging
changes are dated September 2026; they are not reconstructed old tickets.

Raw recordings from friends' phones are a **separate optional data candidate**.
They are not silently included in the code license. No users, revenue, service
accounts, signing keys or production credentials are offered with this corpus.
