# GeoWake alarm tones

The three `geowake_*.wav` files were created on 20 September 2026 by
`tools/sale/generate_alarm_tones.py`, using mathematical sine oscillators and
an amplitude envelope. No recording, sample, or third-party melody was used.
Regenerate them with `python3 tools/sale/generate_alarm_tones.py`.

They are mono, 44.1 kHz, signed 16-bit PCM WAV. The free default is Pulse.
Device loudness and delivery still require physical-device verification.

Earlier revisions contain eleven Samsung/One UI-named recordings for which
no redistribution grant was located. Those files are removed from the
current preparation tree and must not be included in a buyer delivery
or a history export without separate clearance. They remain in the original
repository as historical evidence until a sanitized export is prepared.

This document records the new tones' creation method. It does not change
the license of any earlier third-party recording.
