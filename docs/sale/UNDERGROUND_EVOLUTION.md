# GeoWake underground tracking: evidence-led retrospective

**Archive baseline:** `440e9003cbd3ec7ec78cea0accc56df9cb7d9b2e`, the `production-ready-audit-v2` code lineage. Dates below are recorded Git author dates, not independently verified dates of experiments. Repository paths refer to this baseline unless prefixed with `git show COMMIT:path`. This document describes the work and its limits for an AI-training archive; it does not assert that underground positioning was reliable in general use. No tests were run to prepare this retrospective.

## Founder account, kept separate from repository evidence

The founder reports that the underground EKF remained unreliable, that the approach pivoted toward using IMU/ZUPT evidence to detect train acceleration and deceleration patterns, match them to known stops along a prefetched route, and that procuring enough data to tune the measurementsts cost too much, leading to abandonment of the product. On follow-up, the founder described the recordings as coming from “a couple of friends phones,” said stop anchoring went “nothing beyond experiment,” and supplied no cost figure. These are firsthand accounts supplied for the archive, not Git-established facts. The repository corroborates the technical failure, the stop-anchoring investigation, and the data gap below. It does **not** establish a date for abandonment, a cash amount, a procurement invoice, or that a production-quality stop-anchoring system was completed. Its July 24 data-hunt document proposed free logging apps for self-collection; the founder's cost statement can include the unquantified work of acquiring, labelling, calibrating, and validating rides, but the repository does not measure that cost (`docs/business_os/research/underground_data_hunt.md:7-17,29-35`). The research `SYNTHESIS.md:53-54` describes its two analyzed rides as one-device data; the founder's broader phone description needs ride-level provenance before any device-count claim is generalized.

## The technical problem and chronology

The alarm needed to track progress on a known route when GPS went dark in a tunnel. A useful internal representation is distance `s` along the route, with speed `v`, accelerometer bias, and uncertainty. The safety requirement was to fire by the destination even if the position estimate was poor. The repo eventually used a physics reachability upper bound as the alarm safety net; its loose bound can fire early, while the EKF and station events were intended to improve precision. See `lib/core/reachability/reachability.dart:48-70,892-960`, `lib/core/ekf/ekf_pipeline.dart:449-539`, and `docs/research/underground/DATA_CONTRACT.md:8-14`.

## What the pivot meant

The January implementation already contained ZUPT and a station-association hook. The later pivot was in **what to trust**: direct integration of handheld acceleration into speed or distance was too noisy, while an event meaning “the train stopped” could, if confirmed with very high precision, connect the estimate to a known station's route distance. The July research measured poor handheld stop classification: 38–56% recall, 25–36% precision, with false detections accumulating in the direction that could make an alarm late (`docs/research/underground/EMPIRICAL_RESULTS.md:61-87`). Its brake-event velocity integration also failed on the two rides, with 71–83% distance error (`EMPIRICAL_RESULTS.md:93-122`). A ZUPT says speed is near zero; it does not itself identify **which** station. The implemented pipeline distinguishes velocity correction from position snapping (`lib/core/ekf/ekf_pipeline.dart:449-510`), and the orchestrator attempts a station candidate only after a ZUPT, then applies candidate/confidence checks (`lib/core/ekf/ekf_orchestrator.dart:496-565`; `lib/core/ekf/station_association.dart:106-125,197-209`). The sensor path and callback are wired in `lib/services/sensor_fusion.dart:90-107,269-289` and `lib/services/tracking/location_stream_handler.dart:676-700` when route geometry is available. One important implementation discrepancy remains: degraded-mode `StationAssociation` can choose the nearest station ahead from **multiple** candidates (`station_association.dart:143-177,197-209`), although the orchestrator comment describes a single-candidate gate (`ekf_orchestrator.dart:539-547`). A small post-snap `σ_s` therefore does not by itself prove that the station identity was unambiguous.

The offline route-constrained alternative combined a detected stop with the known station sequence and a between-station motion profile. `EMPIRICAL_RESULTS.md:128-158,188-210` reports a dramatic result in one 549-second blackout and 43–46% less early firing in two evaluated rides. These are **candidate-model results**, conditional on trustworthy stop events. The same research says false stops are the late-fire hazard, only two handheld rides on one device were analyzed, and barometer/magnetometer/WiFi corroboration was missing (`SYNTHESIS.md:8-22,42-54`). `9e61e30` deliberately left the alarm bound unchanged. Some plots are committed under `docs/research/underground/plots/`, but only `core_probe.py`, `e1_signals.py`, and unrelated scale/mutation scripts are present under `docs/research/underground/scripts/`; the full external analysis workspace and both real replay fixtures are not in this Git snapshot, and the cited external workspace was absent from this host during review. The numerical offline results therefore remain repository-authored reports rather than independently reproducible measurements from the sale archive alone.

## Where the investigation stopped

The dated data hunt records no identified public dataset combining real underground train motion, high-rate phone IMU, station-level position/time ground truth, and clean commercial rights. It distinguishes partial substitutes: rail data without metro blackout/dwell coverage, non-transit inertial data, and a paywalled/research-restricted subway dataset lacking station-level ground truth (`docs/business_os/research/underground_data_hunt.md:5-17,37-40`). Its proposed next experiments were to instrument more rides across lines, phone placements, and a parallel-car case; collect barometer and magnetometer streams; align station timestamps; tune ZUPT/association; and validate a synthetic generator against held-out real rides (`underground_data_hunt.md:19-35`; `docs/research/underground/SYNTHESIS.md:45-51`). These were **plans and hypotheses**. The current `sensor_fusion.dart` path exposes accelerometer, gyroscope, and GPS inputs but no barometer logging; `SYNTHESIS.md:53-54` explicitly marks the cross-device and express/skip cases unvalidated. The founder's account places the abandonment decision at this data/calibration bottleneck, without a verifiable date or amount.

## Data and decision flow at the archived baseline

This is a **code-path map**, not a claim that the behavior worked on all rides. The research fixture contract has a different role: it describes how to *evaluate* the code, not live production input (`docs/research/underground/DATA_CONTRACT.md:16-47`).

| Input | Gate / transform / retained state | Output and decision consumer |
|---|---|---|
| Oriented route polyline, metro-leg stop distances, route type | `LocationStreamHandler` constructs `SensorFusionManager` only when route geometry exists and supplies transit-leg stops; `RouteGeometry` maps a GPS point into along-route `s` (`lib/services/tracking/location_stream_handler.dart:676-700`; `lib/services/sensor_fusion.dart:90-107,130-156`). | EKF enabled for that route; station arc-distances provide candidates, not proof of arrival. Missing geometry disables this EKF path (`sensor_fusion.dart:120-128`). |
| GPS lat/lng, horizontal accuracy, speed, timestamp | Project onto route; ignore off-route projections; reject a nearly frozen position if estimated train speed is already >2 m/s; on accepted fixes compute innovation and robust GPS update (`lib/core/ekf/ekf_orchestrator.dart:255-289`; `lib/core/ekf/ekf_pipeline.dart:279-345`). The code initializes `[s,v,b]` from GPS and keeps a 3×3 covariance (`ekf_pipeline.dart:35-71,292-299`). | A real accepted fix corrects EKF progress and can re-anchor the independent reachability upper bound (`lib/services/tracking/alarm_controller.dart:688-710`). This relies on the GPS quality/route projection gates working; those gates are not underground ground truth. |
| Accelerometer, latest gyroscope sample, monotonic stopwatch | `sensor_fusion.dart:247-292` forms timestamped six-axis IMU samples. The orchestrator updates tilt, IMU feature/variance windows, motion class and EKF prediction; it falls back to raw `ax` if tilt output is unavailable (`lib/core/ekf/ekf_orchestrator.dart:133-205`). After >3 s without an accepted on-route fix it signals GPS-unavailable/degraded mode (`sensor_fusion.dart:171-188,269-279`). | Public `s`, `v`, `σ_s`, bias, mode and motion state are emitted (`ekf_pipeline.dart:50-64`; `sensor_fusion.dart:291-292`) and received by tracking code (`location_stream_handler.dart:701-706`; `trackingservice.dart:2393-2408`). For metro or degraded tracking, `trackingservice.dart:1270-1279` prefers finite EKF `s` as alarm progress. The January plan prescribed Android `SensorEvent.timestamp`; this baseline uses its own stopwatch (`git show 7a604a2:docs/ekf_planning/LOCKED_DECISIONS.md`, lines 27-35, versus `sensor_fusion.dart:261-265`): plan and implementation diverged. |
| IMU variance, classified motion, EKF velocity | ZUPT detector has different rules with GPS present and degraded. With GPS present it uses low velocity plus either quiet IMU or stationary class. It is **not** a position measurement, and code explicitly stops forcing `σ_s` down merely because a stop was detected (`lib/core/ekf/ekf_pipeline.dart:449-510`). |
| ZUPT, current `s`/`σ_s`, known station arc-distances, metro-leg flag | Association searches within `3σ_s + adaptive margin`, requires a dwell, and in degraded mode can choose nearest-ahead from multiple matches (`lib/core/ekf/station_association.dart:55-81,106-177,197-209`). ## Parameter and calibration provenance

| Choice in code or research | Recorded basis and limits |
|---|---|
| EKF process/measurement defaults | `EkfConfig` sets `sigmaAccel=0.15`, `sigmaBias=0.001`, GPS variance floor `625 m²`, speed variance `1 (m/s)²`, ZUPT variance `0.0025 (m/s)²`, station variance `100 m²`, position uncertainty floor `5 m`, and bias limit `0.5` (`lib/core/ekf/ekf_types.dart:117-153`). The source contains constants and comments, but this archive does not contain a multi-device calibration report or parameter-fit dataset proving these values generalize. The initial plan's phrase “learn sensor biases” is architecture intent, while the code's GPS-derived acceleration buffer and ZUPT correction are implementation evidence (`git show 7a604a2:docs/ekf_planning/LOCKED_DECISIONS.md`, lines 8-26; `ekf_pipeline.dart:43-48,449-479`). |
| ZUPT thresholds | At `7a604a2`, velocity <0.3 m/s, accelerometer variance <4e-4, gyroscope variance <7.62e-5, and 5 s confirmation were coded (`git show 7a604a2:lib/core/ekf/zupt_detector.dart`, lines 5-55). The baseline uses <0.8 m/s, variances <1.0/<0.40, and 300/800 ms candidate/confirmation defaults; degraded mode tightens to <0.15/<0.05 **with stationary-class corroboration** (`lib/core/ekf/zupt_detector.dart:5-18,50-89`). Code comments and `f956929` link the later rule to particular real-ride failure. They do not establish threshold calibration over phones, placements, routes or vehicle classes. |
| Station association | The early version required one station within `3σ_s+50 m` and 20 s dwell (`git show 7a604a2:lib/core/ekf/station_association.dart`, lines 23-57). The baseline uses 50–150 m adaptive margin, 3 s dwell, and the degraded nearest-choice branch (`lib/core/ekf/station_association.dart:55-81,106-177`). The code comment says a growing `σ_s` should make the margin shrink, but `baseMargin + 0.5σ_s` makes it grow until capped; the overall `3σ_s` window also grows. This is an internal rationale/implementation mismatch, not proof that this branch caused each observed miss. The July 24 commit identifies ambiguity as a **hypothesis** (`b1b30f5`; `docs/business_os/research/underground_data_hunt.md:19-35`). |
| Filter guards after measured failure | `dd730d4` repaired covariance consistency before high-gain ZUPT and added route-envelope/state guards for the replay spike; baseline velocity clamp is ±25 m/s and position covariance can grow to `σ_s=3,000 m` rather than being held at a falsely confident small value (`lib/core/ekf/ekf_pipeline.dart:459-466,610-667`). These are defensive bounds and uncertainty choices, not demonstrated calibration. |
| Alarm speed and time assumptions | `VLineTable` contains nominal metro 28 m/s and faster express/RRTS ceilings; `AlarmController` chooses line-aware ceilings and a monotonic timebase (`lib/core/reachability/reachability.dart:48-70`; `lib/services/tracking/alarm_controller.dart:144-165,353-379`). `SYNTHESIS.md:24-28,42-43` warns that replacing a design ceiling with observed speed can under-bound true speed. The data-hunt and two real rides do not certify every ceiling or a production-wide “never late” guarantee. |
| Offline stop-anchored model | `EMPIRICAL_RESULTS.md:128-158` says its comparison uses `a_max=d_max=0.8 m/s²`, cruise speed 13 m/s, and `V_LINE=22.2 m/s` from GPS aggregate rather than per-window ground truth. These are **research-model parameters**, not the archived `EkfConfig`, nor the default reachability 28 m/s ceiling. The offline 43–46% early-fire reduction (`EMPIRICAL_RESULTS.md:188-210`) should stay attached to those choices and the two rides; it is not evidence of a tuned production detector. |

## Training value and limits

This archive contains a real sequence of model assumptions, diagnostic evidence, corrected code, and unresolved counterexamples. Two concrete before/after exercises are `c556408` → `dd730d4` (covariance-driven EKF spike; replay harness) and `b1b30f5` / pre-fix `d0d76e5` → `f956929` (false ZUPT during GPS-denied cruising; `zupt_degraded_gating_test.dart`). The stop-anchoring idea is an **open research case**, not a completed fix or verified deployment. A training record can cite these commits and paths as sources, clearly label tests as synthetic or external-real, and preserve the negative findings. It must not convert the founder's cost account, offline research numbers, historical commit messages, or skipped real-fixture test into invented tickets, validated product performance, user adoption, or a quantified budget.
