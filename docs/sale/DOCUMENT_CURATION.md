## Suggested archive organization

- 00-current: the seven buyer documents in the reading path, plus the exact revision and path manifest. The claims register points to fresh test/build receipts rather than copying logs into prose.
- 10-source: seller-controlled source at the approved revision, with dependency notices and ringtone provenance. Keep technical build inputs such as linux/CMakeLists.txt as source, not first-pass narrative.
- 20-history: a sanitized, explicitly mapped history; original dated architecture, product, business and website documents grouped as historical context. Do not backdate the September capture or newly written retrospectives.
- 30-research: historical underground hypotheses, simulation results, adversarial findings and dated external-source digests. Keep real-ride-derived reports seller-side until recording provenance and reproducibility are cleared. Do not promote generated scenarios into recorded journeys.
- 40-derived: regenerate .wake for the approved sanitized tree and scan its source snippets and paths for secrets and restricted content. An older snapshot may be retained seller-side with its original manifest and revision. Build logs, probe files and template boilerplate are not current evidence.
- 90-withheld: a manifest of excluded paths and reasons, not the withheld bytes. docs/sale/RIGHTS_AND_EXCLUSIONS.md:11-21 already identifies prior third-party audio, mixed map data, raw rides, derived geometry and historical credentials. document-curation.csv specifically withholds docs/research/raw/flagged_lines.md, docs/research/underground/DATA_CONTRACT.md and scripts/extract_log.txt from the default document delivery. Their engineering context remains summarized in the cleared buyer notes.

This audit did not validate current external law, platform policy, Maps prices, Play billing, device alarm delivery, or rights to raw ride/third-party data. The next document pass should be tied to the final captured-and-sanitized revision and its verification receipts.
